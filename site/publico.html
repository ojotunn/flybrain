Under the hood

Connectome-constrained leaky integrate-and-fire simulation of the adult Drosophila brain (Shiu et al. 2024; engine derived from fly-brain, MIT). Nothing is scripted: every flash is a neuron crossing threshold. The model has no spontaneous activity, so between inputs she is silent, and no fatigue, so a runaway state is possible; when it happens she blacks out, comes back, and it is counted.

Her body is the NeuroMechFly model with recorded fly leg trajectories; rhythm and direction come from her descending neurons. Takeoff follows the giant fiber. Wingbeat is stylized. Only the antenna twitches and head glances at rest are physiology rather than decisions.

Market: Pons trades become her senses by a fixed public map (buy = sugar, sell = bitter, big sell = shadow, many trades = vibration). Her motor neurons become orders by fixed rules (proboscis = buy, escape or backing up = sell all, lasting bitter = sell a quarter). No AI agent anywhere: she has reflexes, not a strategy. She never trades her own token.
